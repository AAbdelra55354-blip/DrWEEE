Introduction

Transforming E-Waste into Opportunity

DR.WEEE Company is a specialized leader in electronic waste recycling, extracting valuable materials including basic metals such as iron and copper, precious metals like gold and palladium, and safely managing hazardous elements such as lead and cadmium. Our comprehensive business model addresses all market needs related to electronic waste management through collection, sorting, dismantling, and advanced recycling processes.
