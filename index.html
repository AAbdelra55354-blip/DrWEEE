Still have a question?
What does DR.WEEE do?

We provide responsible e-waste collection, material recovery, circular IT solutions, and sustainable printing services for businesses and communities.

How can I arrange an e-waste collection?

Send us your details through the contact form with the type and approximate volume of equipment. Our team will review your request and contact you with the next steps.

What kinds of electronic waste do you accept?

We handle a wide range of IT and electronic equipment. Acceptance depends on the item, condition, quantity, and safe handling requirements, which we confirm during assessment.

Do you offer partnerships or franchise opportunities?

Yes. We work with organizations, collection partners, and franchise operators. Visit our services page or contact us to discuss a model that fits your market.

What happens to the materials after collection?

Collected materials are assessed, sorted, and directed through responsible recovery or reuse pathways. We prioritize data security, traceability, and minimizing landfill waste.
