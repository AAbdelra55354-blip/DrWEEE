Why E-Waste Recycling Matters

Every piece of electronic waste you recycle makes a real difference for our planet's future

1

Reduces Greenhouse Gases

Manufacturing new electronics requires mining raw materials and energy-intensive processes. Recycling prevents 50-80% of the CO₂ emissions compared to producing new devices.

2

Conserves Natural Resources

One million recycled laptops save the energy equivalent of powering 3,500 homes for a year. Remanufactured cartridges save up to 3.5 pounds of oil per cartridge!

3

Prevents Toxic Pollution

E-waste contains harmful materials like lead and mercury. Proper recycling prevents these toxins from contaminating soil and water, protecting ecosystems and human health.

4

Supports Circular Economy

Remanufactured printer cartridges use 70% less energy than new ones and keep plastic out of landfills. Each cartridge can be reused 4-5 times before recycling!

5

Creates Green Jobs

The recycling industry creates 10 times more jobs than landfills. Your participation supports sustainable employment and helps build a green economy.

6

Protects Climate Goals

If everyone recycled their electronics, we could reduce global e-waste emissions by 50 million tons annually - equivalent to taking 10 million cars off the road!
